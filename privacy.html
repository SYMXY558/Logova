Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Logova ("the app", "we") is a puzzle game. We built it to collect as little data as possible. This policy explains exactly what is collected, why, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted.

What we don't collect

Logova has no accounts, no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking. We never ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and we never sell or share data for advertising.

Data stored only on your device

Your puzzle progress, statistics, streak and settings are saved on your device and are not sent to us. If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device; no push token or notification data leaves your phone.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Purchases of Logova+ are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

To unlock and restore Logova+, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service provider. When the app starts, the RevenueCat SDK sends RevenueCat:

  • an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID (not linked to your name or Apple ID),
  • your App Store purchase and subscription history (from Apple's receipts),
  • your device's vendor identifier (IDFV), device model, iOS version and app version,
  • your preferred language and App Store country.

This information is used only to provide, verify and restore your purchases and to produce aggregated subscription statistics (for example, how many people subscribe). It is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Third parties

The only third parties that receive data are Apple (to process payments) and RevenueCat (described above). They act as service providers and are required to protect your data with protections at least equal to those in this policy. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: deleted when you delete the app, or anytime via Settings → Reset all progress.
  • Purchase records at RevenueCat: kept while your purchases need to be verified or restored, and as long as required for accounting and legal obligations.

To request deletion of the purchase data RevenueCat holds for your device, email us at matthewgerdes001@gmail.com. We will delete it through RevenueCat's customer-deletion tools and confirm by email, usually within 30 days. Purchase records that Apple keeps are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Logova is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
