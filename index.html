FAQ

How do I restore Logova+ on a new device?
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Logova, or tap Manage subscription in the app.

When do new puzzles arrive?
Every day at midnight, your local time. Everyone gets the same puzzles.

How do I delete my data?
Your progress lives only on your phone — delete the app or use Settings → Reset all progress. For purchase records, see the Privacy Policy.
